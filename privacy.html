Privacy

This site makes watercolour caricature keepsakes for guests at Pooja & Amit's wedding. It is a private, non-commercial project.

What happens to your photo

  • Your photo is sent to an AI painting service only to make your caricature.
  • On the wedding day, your finished keepsake and your photo may be saved to Pooja & Amit's private Google Drive, so they can keep the memories. They are not shared publicly or sold.
  • Your name and message appear only on your keepsake.

Google Drive access

The site uses Google's "drive.file" permission on Pooja & Amit's own account only: it can see and create only the files and folders it made itself, nothing else in that Drive. Guests never sign in.

Questions or removal

Email pooja.and.amit.wedding@gmail.com and we'll delete your photo and keepsake.
